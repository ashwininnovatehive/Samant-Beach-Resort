The Hidden Gem of Bhogwe

A quiet shore the Konkan kept to itself.

Tucked into Kolwel Wadi at Bhogwe, where the Karli River slips into the Arabian Sea beneath the ramparts of Nivati Fort, Samant Beach Resort & Villa sits on a 3 km stretch of virgin sand, sheltered by rustling coconut groves.

Mornings begin with fishing boats on the horizon and a home-cooked breakfast; afternoons drift by on the backwaters; evenings belong to salt air, a crackling bonfire and a sky that turns terracotta, then indigo. With only nine handcrafted cottages, the beach stays yours.

Konkani & Malvani Kitchen

Today's catch, grandmother's recipes.

Fish is bought off the boats each morning and cooked the Malvani way: fresh-ground coconut, kokum, and a masala that took generations to perfect. Meals are served under the palms with the sea at your feet.

  1. 01

    Fresh Catch of the Day

    Pomfret, bangda & kingfish, tawa-fried or grilled on the beach.

  2. 02

    Malvani Fish Curry & Solkadhi

    Fiery coconut curry cooled by a pink kokum-coconut digestive.

  3. 03

    Rava-Fried Surmai & Prawns

    Semolina-crusted, crisp outside and succulent within.

  4. 04

    Homemade Konkani Breakfast

    Poha, ghavne, amboli & chai, served warm on the veranda.

Experiences & Nearby

Days that write their own stories.

From dolphins at dawn to forts at dusk, the Sindhudurg coast is on your doorstep. Our team will happily arrange boats, guides and transfers.

Sea · Early morning

Dolphin Spotting Safari

Set out at sunrise from the Karli confluence to meet playful Indo-Pacific humpback dolphins in calm morning waters.

Sea · Malvan

Scuba & Snorkeling

Discover the reefs around Malvan and Sindhudurg Fort with certified instructors. No experience needed.

Heritage · Walkable

Nivati Fort Trek

Climb to the Maratha-era ramparts for sweeping views over Bhogwe, the river mouth and the open sea.

Adventure · Tarkarli

Water Sports

Parasailing, jet-ski, banana rides and kayaking at Tarkarli & Devbag, a short hop across the river.

Nature · Golden hour

Karli Backwater Cruise

Glide past mangroves, fishing hamlets and sandbars where the Karli River meets the Arabian Sea.

Nature · Guided

Jungle & Grove Treks

Walk hidden trails through cashew orchards, coconut groves and Konkan village life with a local guide.

Heritage · Malvan

Sindhudurg Sea Fort

Take a ferry to Shivaji Maharaj's island fortress, built in 1664 and still standing guard over Malvan.

On property · Evenings

Bonfire & Stargazing

End the day with a crackling beach bonfire, grilled seafood and a sky full of stars.
